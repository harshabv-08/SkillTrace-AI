ABOUT SKILLTRACE AI

Career analysis built around evidence.

SkillTrace AI combines resume text extraction, skill detection, keyword analysis and TF-IDF based text similarity to produce an explainable resume assessment. The score is an ATS-style estimate, not a score from any particular employer's applicant tracking system.
